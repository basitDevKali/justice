Justice Module

Two contexts, Court and Prison. A verdict in the Court creates the imprisonment in the Prison. Both contexts connect to the citizens and finances of Hannes's modules and to Jobs and Complaint Management in Julia's Operations & Safety module.

One row = one imprisonment, at most one per court case. The row is created only through PronounceVerdict_action, which opens the EditView prefilled (no "New" button anywhere) · an inmate is shown as citizen name + CASENUMBER of its court case · CITIZEN_ID is taken over from the court case and read-only · CELL is picked from a dropdown 001–100 and suggested automatically (see "CELL suggestion" below) · ADMISSIONDATE defaults to today, not before the day of VERDICTDATE (compare days only, VERDICTDATE also has a time) · PLANNEDRELEASEDATE = ADMISSIONDATE + SENTENCEMONTHS of the case, read-only · SECURITYLEVEL → Keyword SecurityLevel, defaults to MEDIUM · STATUS → Keyword InmateStatus, defaults to IN_CUSTODY · STATUS, RELEASEDATE and JOB_ID are set only by the actions (read-only in the EditView) · once STATUS is RELEASED or RELEASED_EARLY, the whole record is read-only.

Read-only after the verdict (J_COURTCASE)

  • From STATUS = SENTENCED or CLOSED: CASETYPE, CITIZEN_ID and COMPLAINT_ID are read-only, so they can't contradict the verdict or the inmate
  • Still editable: TITLE, JUDGE, and RECEIVABLE_ID (FINE only)

Validations

onValidation on CITIZEN_ID, J_INMATE

  • A citizen can be IN_CUSTODY only once at a time.
  • Error if another J_INMATE row of the same citizen has STATUS = IN_CUSTODY.
  • Only relevant for a new inmate, because CITIZEN_ID is read-only afterwards. PronounceVerdict_action already blocks this; the validation is the safety net, e.g. when two cases of the same citizen get a PRISON verdict at the same time.
  • Message: "{CITIZEN} is already in custody (case {CASENUMBER})."

onValidation on CELL and SECURITYLEVEL, J_INMATE

  • A cell holds at most 2 inmates; an inmate with SECURITYLEVEL = HIGH always gets a cell of their own.
  • Looks at the other inmates in the chosen CELL with STATUS = IN_CUSTODY (the inmate being edited is not counted). Error if:
    • one of them is HIGH: "Cell {CELL} is occupied by an inmate with security level HIGH."
    • there are already 2: "Cell {CELL} is full. Please choose a different cell."
    • this inmate is HIGH and the cell is not empty: "An inmate with security level HIGH needs a cell of their own."
  • The same check runs on both fields, because changing either one can break the rule.
  • SECURITYLEVEL only: HIGH is not allowed while the inmate has a job: "Remove the job before setting security level HIGH."

Actions

PronounceVerdict_action links Gericht → Gefängnis

On J_COURTCASE
  • Allowed only while STATUS = FILED
  • Dialog: pick VERDICT (choices depend on CASETYPE). FINE also asks for FINEAMOUNT, PRISON for SENTENCEMONTHS
  • ACQUITTAL, FINE, PROBATION: saves VERDICT, VERDICTDATE = now (and FINEAMOUNT for FINE) and sets STATUS = CLOSED
  • PRISON, citizen already in custody: error, the case stays FILED. The verdict can be pronounced after the release
  • PRISON, otherwise: opens the inmate EditView. It gets the case, CITIZEN_ID, SENTENCEMONTHS and the verdict date as parameters, because the case itself is not changed yet. The clerk picks SECURITYLEVEL and saves
  • On saving the inmate (onDBInsert), the case gets VERDICT = PRISON, VERDICTDATE, SENTENCEMONTHS and STATUS = SENTENCED. If the form is cancelled, nothing is saved and the case stays FILED

AssignJob_action links Gefängnis → Jobs

On J_INMATE
  • Allowed only for STATUS = IN_CUSTODY and SECURITYLEVEL ≠ HIGH
  • Lookup shows only open jobs of the Jobs module
  • Writes the selected job to JOB_ID; an empty selection removes the job

ReleaseInmate_action

On J_INMATE
  • Allowed only for STATUS = IN_CUSTODY
  • Started manually by the clerk (no background job), after a confirmation question, because the release cannot be undone
  • Sets RELEASEDATE = today and STATUS = RELEASED_EARLY if today is before PLANNEDRELEASEDATE, otherwise RELEASED
  • Sets the linked case from SENTENCED to CLOSED
  • The cell is free again automatically, because the cell validation counts only IN_CUSTODY

Calculated Display Values

remainingDays (No database column needed!)

On J_INMATE, SQL expression (MariaDB), read-only, shown while STATUS = IN_CUSTODY
GREATEST(DATEDIFF(J_INMATE.PLANNEDRELEASEDATE, CURRENT_DATE), 0)

Example: admission 2026-09-01, 6 months → planned release 2027-03-01. On 2026-10-12 the view shows 140 days.

Out of Scope

Correcting or appealing a verdict

A pronounced verdict is final in this module. There is no action to revoke or change it, because that would also have to undo the J_INMATE row. The Log tab shows who pronounced which verdict and when.
