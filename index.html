Justice Module

Two contexts, Court and Prison. A verdict in the Court creates the imprisonment in the Prison. Both contexts connect to the citizens and finances of Hannes's modules and to Jobs and Complaint Management in Julia's Operations & Safety module.

Keyword Categories

How keyword values are stored

1 · CaseType

CRIMINALADMINISTRATIVE

CRIMINAL allows every verdict. ADMINISTRATIVE (e.g. an escalated complaint) allows only ACQUITTAL or FINE.

2 · CaseStatus

FILEDVERDICTCLOSED

VERDICT is used only while a prison sentence is running. Every other verdict closes the case at once.

3 · VerdictType

ACQUITTALFINEPROBATIONPRISON

FINE creates a receivable in Finanzen, PRISON creates a J_INMATE row.

4 · Judge

WeberKrügerAydin

Judges are added as keyword entries in the keyword administration. Fixed name keywords are fragile in a real system (judges come and go) — fine for this training project, and no validation depends on specific judge names.

5 · SecurityLevel

LOWMEDIUMHIGH

Defaults to MEDIUM. HIGH means a cell of one's own (cell validation) and no work assignment (AssignJob_action).

6 · InmateStatus

IN_CUSTODYRELEASED_EARLYRELEASED

RELEASED_EARLY = released before PLANNEDRELEASEDATE. Both release values are final.

Validations

onValidation on CITIZEN_ID, J_INMATE

  • A citizen can have only one J_INMATE row with STATUS = IN_CUSTODY at a time.
  • The row being edited is excluded from the check (J_INMATEID <> current), so saving an existing inmate does not fail on itself.
  • Message: "{CITIZEN} is already in custody (case {CASENUMBER})."

onValidation on CELL, J_INMATE

  • At most 2 inmates with STATUS = IN_CUSTODY per CELL.
  • An inmate with SECURITYLEVEL = HIGH never shares a cell: neither can a HIGH inmate move into an occupied cell, nor anyone into a cell with a HIGH inmate.
  • The inmate being edited is not counted (J_INMATEID <> current); otherwise editing an inmate in a full cell would fail.
  • Runs on SECURITYLEVEL as well, because raising an inmate to HIGH changes whether the cell may be shared.
  • Message: "Cell {CELL} is full. Please choose a different cell."

Actions

PronounceVerdict_action links Gericht → Gefängnis + Finanzen

On J_COURTCASE
  • Allowed only while STATUS = FILED
  • Dialog: pick VERDICT (choices depend on CASETYPE). FINE also asks for FINEAMOUNT, PRISON for SENTENCEMONTHS
  • Saves VERDICT and VERDICTDATE = now
  • FINE: Finanzen creates a bill, its id goes into RECEIVABLE_ID → STATUS = CLOSED
  • PRISON: error if the citizen is already in custody. Otherwise STATUS = VERDICT and the inmate form opens prefilled; the clerk picks SECURITYLEVEL and saves. If the form is cancelled, the "New" button in the Imprisonment tab opens it again
  • ACQUITTAL / PROBATION: STATUS = CLOSED

AssignJob_action links Gefängnis → Jobs

On J_INMATE
  • Allowed only for STATUS = IN_CUSTODY and SECURITYLEVEL ≠ HIGH
  • Lookup shows only open jobs of the Jobs module
  • Writes the selected job to JOB_ID

ReleaseInmate_action

On J_INMATE
  • Started manually by the clerk, there is no background job. Due and overdue releases are listed in the "Justice today" dashlet
  • Writes today's date to RELEASEDATE
  • Allowed only for STATUS = IN_CUSTODY
  • Before PLANNEDRELEASEDATE: STATUS = RELEASED_EARLY, otherwise STATUS = RELEASED
  • Frees the cell (the cell validation counts only IN_CUSTODY) and sets the linked case to STATUS = CLOSED

Calculated Display Values

remainingDays (No database column needed!)

On J_INMATE, read-only, shown while STATUS = IN_CUSTODY
SELECT GREATEST(DATEDIFF(PLANNEDRELEASEDATE, CURRENT_DATE), 0)
FROM   J_INMATE
WHERE  J_INMATEID = :currentInmate

Example: admission 01/09/2026, 6 months → planned release 01/03/2027. On 12/10/2026 the view shows 140 days.

Requests Between Modules

J_CriminalRecord_rest offered

In plain words: "Has this person ever been convicted?"

Another module asks with: a citizen

Justice answers with the citizen's prior convictions and whether they are in custody right now.

Example answer: not in custody right now, 1 conviction:

CASEVERDICTDATE
J-2026-0004PROBATION17/04/2026

DATE is the day of the verdict (the full VERDICTDATE timestamp is returned in ISO format, e.g. 2026-04-17T10:30:00; consumers display only the day). Used by Jobs (background check of applicants) and Bürger (certificate of good conduct).

J_CourtCase_rest offered

In plain words: "Please open a court case for this complaint."

Another module asks Justice to: create a court case

Complaint Management escalates a complaint to court. It sends COMPLAINT_ID, CITIZEN_ID (the person the complaint is about, not the person who complained) and TITLE. The case starts with CASETYPE = ADMINISTRATIVE and STATUS = FILED, and the response returns the CASENUMBER.

Finanzen · createReceivable needed from Hannes

In plain words: "Please send this citizen a bill for the fine." A receivable is a bill that someone still has to pay.

Justice asks Finanzen, from PronounceVerdict_action

Justice sends CITIZEN_ID, the amount and a reason ("Fine J-2026-0012"). Finanzen creates the bill and answers with its id, which Justice stores in RECEIVABLE_ID.

Out of Scope

Correcting or appealing a verdict

A pronounced verdict is final in this module. There is no action to revoke or change it, because that would also have to undo the receivable in Finanzen and the J_INMATE row. The Log tab shows who pronounced which verdict and when.
